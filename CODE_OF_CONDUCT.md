# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want Canton Streams to be a respectful, practical, and welcoming open-source project. Contributors, maintainers, and users should be able to collaborate without harassment or hostility.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful in reviews, issues, discussions, and chat.
- Assume good intent, but stay clear and direct when giving technical feedback.
- Focus criticism on code, behavior, and design decisions, not on people.
- Welcome questions from newer contributors and document decisions where helpful.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or sustained personal attacks
- Discriminatory or demeaning language related to personal characteristics
- Doxxing or sharing private information without permission
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces
- Trolling, baiting, or deliberately derailing technical discussion

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project-controlled spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation reviews, and other official community channels.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:

- Use the hosting platform's moderation or reporting features when available.
- Contact the project maintainers or repository owner privately.
- If the report also includes a security concern, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will review reports promptly and may take any action they deem appropriate, including warnings, comment removal, temporary restrictions, or bans.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this policy. Repeated or severe violations may lead to removal from community spaces or rejection of future contributions.
